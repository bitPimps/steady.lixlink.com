Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Steady ("the app") is built so there's very little to say in a privacy policy. We don't run servers for the app, we don't operate an account system, and we don't collect the data you track. This page explains what that means. It also covers the basic analytics on this website.

What Steady stores, and where

Steady stores everything you enter (habit names, start dates, daily check-ins, relapse logs, notes, and settings) locally on your device, in a database that stays on your phone by default. We have no server that receives, processes, or stores this information, and no way to access it.

Optional cloud backup

Premium users can turn on cloud backup. When it's on, Steady encrypts your data on your device before uploading it to your own iCloud (iOS) or Google Drive (Android) account. You control that storage, and Apple's or Google's privacy terms govern it. We can't read the backup, because it's encrypted before it leaves your device and we don't hold the keys.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export a plain-text copy of everything you've logged from Settings → Privacy at any time. You can also permanently delete all habit and check-in data from the same screen. Both actions happen entirely on your device.

Accounts and identity

Steady doesn't ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information. There's no login and no account system.

Purchases

You buy Premium through Apple's App Store or Google Play, and Apple or Google processes the purchase under its own privacy policy. We use RevenueCat to validate your purchase and manage your entitlement across devices; RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and a device identifier to do this, under its own privacy policy. Steady only learns whether you hold an active entitlement, not your payment details.

Notifications

Steady schedules milestone and check-in reminders locally, using your phone's own notification system. No notification content goes through a push server, because Steady doesn't operate one.

Analytics and advertising

The Steady app doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and it doesn't track how you use it. Nothing in the app is sent to Google Analytics.

This website works differently. It uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), loaded through Google Tag Manager, for basic traffic measurement: which pages get visited, roughly where visitors are, what device and browser they use, and how they found the site. Google may set cookies to do this. We don't use the data to identify you, and we don't run ads on the site. Google's handling of the data falls under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Children's privacy

Steady isn't directed at children. The app collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future feature changes how data is handled, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Send questions about this policy or how Steady handles data to steady-support@lixlink.com.
