Sobriety Guides

Updated November 27, 2026

Straight answers to the questions people ask most about tracking sobriety: how to keep your sober date, what to expect in the early months, what to do after a slip, and how to choose an app. Written by the team behind Steady, a private sobriety and habit tracker.

  • How to Keep Track of Your Sobriety Date

    Pick your date, then track it on paper, in a calendar, with a spreadsheet formula, or with an app that counts for you.
