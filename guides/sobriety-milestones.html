Sobriety Milestones: 24 Hours to 1 Year and Beyond

By the Steady team · Published November 27, 2026 · Updated November 27, 2026

Short answer: Common sobriety milestones are 24 hours, 1 week, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 9 months, 1 year, and every year after. Many 12-step groups hand out chips or coins at some of them. Steady marks 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 and 180 days and every anniversary with a notification on your phone.

The common sobriety milestones

Each milestone counts from your sobriety date. Here's when each one lands for someone whose sobriety date is January 1, 2026:

MilestoneDate (sober since January 1, 2026)
24 hoursJanuary 2, 2026
1 weekJanuary 8, 2026
2 weeksJanuary 15, 2026
30 daysJanuary 31, 2026
60 daysMarch 2, 2026
90 daysApril 1, 2026
6 months (180 days)June 30, 2026
9 months (270 days)September 28, 2026
1 yearJanuary 1, 2027 (the anniversary)

    Why milestones matter

    Early recovery is long stretches of ordinary days. Milestones break it into goals you can actually see: one week feels reachable on day 3 in a way that one year doesn't. They also give you a moment to look back at what's changed, such as sleep, money, mood and relationships, and to mark it.

    The early ones come quickly on purpose. Twenty-four hours, one week and 30 days fall in the hardest stretch for most people, when encouragement helps most. See What is the hardest month of sobriety? for what to expect in that time.

    Ways to celebrate a sobriety milestone

    • Spend part of the money you've saved on something you'll keep or remember.
    • Share it with someone who's been in your corner, or with your group.
    • Write a note to yourself about what's different since day one.
    • Book a meal, a trip or an experience you'd have skipped before.
    • Pick a new goal: the next milestone, or a skill or habit to build.

    How Steady marks your milestones

    Steady counts from the day you pick and shows your next milestone and how many days away it is on the home screen. Your phone schedules a notification for 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 and 180 days and for every anniversary after that. With Premium, the history screen lists the milestones you've reached alongside your current and longest streak. While app lock is on, milestone notifications don't name your habit.

    What if you slip before a milestone?

    Your milestones follow your current streak, so they restart from your new date. The time you were sober still counts. Read Does a relapse reset your sobriety date? for how to handle it.

    Common questions

    What are the main sobriety milestones?

    24 hours, 1 week, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 9 months, 1 year, and each year after that. Some people also mark 2 weeks, 100 days or 18 months.

    How do I work out my 90 days sober date?

    Add 90 days to your sobriety date. If your sobriety date is January 1, 2026, you reach 90 days on April 1, 2026. The calculator on this page works it out for any date.

    When is my 1 year sober anniversary?

    One year after your sobriety date, on the same calendar day. Sober since January 1, 2026 means your first anniversary is January 1, 2027.

    Do milestones reset after a relapse?

    In most tracking, yes: they follow your current streak. In Steady, logging a relapse reschedules milestone notifications from the new start, and with Premium your past streaks stay in your history.
