How to Keep Track of Your Sobriety Date

By the Steady team · Published November 27, 2026 · Updated November 27, 2026

Short answer: Choose your sobriety date (usually the first full day without drinking or using), write it down, and let something count from it. A sobriety app such as Steady counts the days, hours and minutes for you, reminds you at each milestone and keeps the date safe on your phone. A calendar or a one-line spreadsheet formula also works.

What counts as your sobriety date?

Your sobriety date (or sober date) is the day your current stretch of not drinking or using began. Most people use the first full day without it: if your last drink was on the night of March 3, your sobriety date is March 4. Some recovery groups count from the day of the last drink instead. Neither is wrong. What matters is that you pick one rule and keep using it, so your day count and milestones stay consistent.

If you can't remember the exact day, choose your best estimate. A date you're sure of to within a day or two is good enough to track, and most tracking apps let you correct it later.

How to count your sober days with a spreadsheet

In Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers, put your sobriety date in cell A1 and this formula in cell B1:

=TODAY()-A1

Format B1 as a plain number, not a date. It shows how many full days have passed since your sobriety date and updates every time you open the sheet. For a date 30 days ago it shows 30.

What happens to your sobriety date after a slip?

Most people start a new sobriety date after a relapse, but the days you were sober still happened. See Does a relapse reset your sobriety date? for how to handle it, and how Steady keeps your past streaks.

Common questions

Is my sobriety date the day of my last drink or the day after?

Most people use the day after their last drink, the first full sober day. Some groups count from the last drink itself. Pick one rule and stick to it so your milestones stay consistent.

How do I calculate how many days sober I am?

Subtract your sobriety date from today's date. In a spreadsheet, put the date in A1 and use =TODAY()-A1. A sobriety app such as Steady shows the count automatically, down to the second.

Can I change my sobriety date in an app?

In Steady you can. Open the habit, tap Edit and pick a different start day. Your milestone reminders move to match the new date.

Can I set a sobriety date in the past?

Yes. When you start tracking in Steady, pick the day you actually started, even if it was months ago. The clock counts from the start of that day.
