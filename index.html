Privacy by design

Your data stays on your phone. That's not a setting — it's the architecture.

Steady doesn't have a server, an account system, or a database with your name on it. There's nothing to breach and nothing to sell, because there's nothing we hold in the first place.

  • No sign-up, no email address, no account — ever.
  • Everything you log lives only on this device, by default.
  • Optional backup is encrypted on your phone before it's stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive.
  • Export a plain-text copy of everything you've logged, any time.
  • Delete every habit and check-in permanently, whenever you want.
